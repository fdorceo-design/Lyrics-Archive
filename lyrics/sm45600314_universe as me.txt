-universe as me-

Early Days.
Everything.
When my bonus came in,
I was at the center of everything—
the universe.

Reward.
Unaware the well would run dry,
I drank it all.
When it did, I was furious.

Is there anything you can use that won't run out?
The sun?
My life? Eat, sleep—wake again, right?
Will there come a day I don't wake up?
I'd rather not think about it.

即使我会犯错、会哭泣、并不完美，
我仍是这个宇宙的一部分；
即便我不是宇宙的全部，
我亦与宇宙同体。
你亦是宇宙的一部分，
与我同属一个宇宙。

Creí que el mundo era nuestro.
Y entiendo tan poco.
O nosotros, o el mundo: equivocados.
¿Quizá ambos?
Sea como sea, la existencia sigue.
Todo va a estar bien.

게으른 건 아니지?
우린 노력했어.
그래도 부족해?
아직도 그래?
그게 불가능해?
그럴 리 없어.
분명히 잘 될 거야.
분명히 잘 될 거야.

Even though I make mistakes,
cry,
and am imperfect,
I'm still a part of this universe.
Even though I am not the whole of the universe,
I'm one with it.
You,
too,
are part of the universe,
and belong to the same—
universe as me.

and belong to the same—
yes!
universe as me.
